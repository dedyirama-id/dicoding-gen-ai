# Data

The RAG corpus is made up of four Indonesian regulations (PDF, ~105 MB in total). The PDFs are not committed. [02_rag_legal_assistant.ipynb](../notebooks/02_rag_legal_assistant.ipynb) downloads them to `/content/data` on Colab from the [course's Google Drive folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1LHZ1IncPmmUN5kytFu3i7MoaafFrKDql?usp=sharing) with `gdown`.

| File | Regulation |
| --- | --- |
| `UU Nomor 6 Tahun 2023.pdf` | Law 6/2023: enactment of Perppu 2/2022 on Job Creation (*Cipta Kerja*) as law |
| `PP Nomor 35 Tahun 2021.pdf` | Government Regulation 35/2021: fixed-term contracts, outsourcing, working and rest hours, and termination |
| `PP Nomor 5 Tahun 2021.pdf` | Government Regulation 5/2021: risk-based business licensing |
| `PP Nomor 51 Tahun 2023.pdf` | Government Regulation 51/2023: amendment to Government Regulation 36/2021 on wages |
